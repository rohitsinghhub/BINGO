How to Play

1
Choose Mode

PLAY OFFLINE (same device) ya CREATE/JOIN ROOM (online) select karo.

2
Fill Your Grid

Apne 5×5 grid me 1 se 25 numbers place karo. Random ya 1-to-25 auto-fill bhi use kar sakte ho.

3
Choose First Turn

Decide karo kaun pehla move karega — My Turn ya Opponent.

4
Mark Numbers

Apne turn pe koi bhi number tap karo. Green = your mark, Red = opponent's mark.

5
Win the Game

5 complete lines (row, column, ya diagonal) banao aur BINGO! 🏆

About BINGO 5×5

BINGO 5×5 ek modern paper-replacement bingo game hai, jisme aap dost ke saath same device pe ya online room me khel sakte ho.

Traditional bingo ka classic 5×5 format, clean UI, real-time turn system, aur multiple winning lines detection ke saath.
